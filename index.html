Researcher · Machine Learning & Statistics

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi. I received my PhD in Data Science from Skoltech, Moscow, where I was advised by Maxim Panov (primary supervisor) and Eric Moulines (co-supervisor).

I am a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning and statistics, with a focus on making probabilistic models more reliable and trustworthy. My work spans uncertainty quantification, Bayesian deep learning, federated learning, and conformal prediction.
